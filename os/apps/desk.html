🗂️ Desk

Physics desk experiment. Gestures are the verbs, space is the noun.

  • Scrunch — on empty desk, draw a slow loop around a few files and let go. Close the loop and pull inward to squeeze live.
  • Fan out — hold ~0.3s on a pile (or grab its outer edge), then drag outward.
  • Leaf — drag sideways across a pile's face. Slow = one card per 40px; a flick skims several.
  • Shove — fast swipe (≈120ms) on empty desk. Topples piles and breaks clusters.
  • Tidy — T gathers one pile per type.
  • Pile centre: slow drag = move the pile · quick non-sideways yank = pull the top card off · sideways = Leaf. Double-tap fans. Widgets never join piles.
